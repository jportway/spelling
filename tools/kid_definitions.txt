# Child-friendly definitions, used in place of the WordNet gloss.
#
# WordNet is written for adults. "Feline mammal usually having thick soft fur
# and no ability to roar" is a correct definition of a cat and a useless thing
# to say to a seven year old. These are the words most likely to come up, in
# words she will understand, and they are what the game reads aloud.
#
# It also fixes the handful of common words where WordNet picks a strange
# sense: a bike is not a motorbike, a mug is not a gullible person, and a bear
# is an animal rather than the verb "to bear".
#
# Format: one "word = definition" per line. Lines starting with # are ignored.
# Add your own freely — the game picks them up next time you run
# tools/build_dictionary.py.
